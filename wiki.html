SCP-8707 — “THE ARCADE”

LEVEL 4/8707 FILE. Mirror synchronization fault detected. Memetic access controls are unavailable.

LocationSite-192, Sublevel 4, Deep Storage

Special Containment Procedures

SCP-8707 is held in a five-metre seamless containment cell surrounded by four Scranton Reality Anchors. No powered cable may cross the Sector-4 boundary. Personnel entering the chamber must remain physically tethered and may not face away from the cabinet.

Description

The cabinet contains no conventional processor, display tube, wiring loom, or storage device. Its screen remains luminous without power. The coin slot terminates in a topological dead end; inserted matter cannot be recovered.

When a subject approaches within 1.5 metres, the screen renders a simplified plan of the surrounding room. Control input is reproduced as physical displacement in local space. The device appears to interpret nearby reality as a two-dimensional system governed by its controls.

Incident 8707-C4 // 1991-09-29

During the final PROJECT ARCADE test, local geometry collapsed for 184 seconds. One researcher disappeared, the test chamber suffered structural failure, and SCP-8707 remained undamaged. All contact testing was suspended. Research equipment and an RX-1715 magnetic-media terminal were transferred to SAFE-HOUSE-04.
